01 — The app

The Itinerists, running right here

Angular 19 and TypeScript on a real-time Firestore backend, shipped as a responsive web app and a Capacitor iOS build: authentication, security rules, invite-and-join trip sharing, 20+ lazy-loaded feature pages, and shared expenses converted at payment-date exchange rates.

It started as a single-trip prototype. I re-architected it into a multi-trip platform and wrote the migrations that carried live user data across without breaking a single itinerary. It is still in active development — 12 travellers have used it live across three trips, and four of them are testing it right now, telling me what to fix next.

CI runs 375 unit tests, 158 Firestore security-rules tests and 20 end-to-end tests on every change. Profiling and prefetching cut median sign-in-to-home time by 28%, from 1.82s to 1.31s on a simulated iPhone over 4G, and flight times are tested across all 418 IANA time zones.

The phone runs a demo build seeded with a sample trip, so there is nothing to sign in to. Add, edit and reorder whatever you like; it all lives only in this session.
